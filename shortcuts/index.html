Family shortcut

Two steps per phone, about two minutes. After that, everything with a date in Family Organizer lands in this iPhone's Calendar and Reminders (list "Family") by itself.

Step 1 · Install Family Papers

If Family Papers is already on the phone, tap Replace (not Add), so there is only one.

Install Family Papers (English, Janek) Instalar Family Papers (español, Arlette)

Step 2 · Turn on the calendar sync

Apple does not let any link or shortcut create an automation, so this one step is done by hand, once per phone.

  1. Open Shortcuts, tap Automation in the bottom bar, then +.
  2. Choose Email.
  3. Subject Contains: [FO-SYNC] Janek on Janek's phone, [FO-SYNC] Arlette on Arlette's phone.
  4. Account: the familiapig account.
  5. Choose Run Immediately, tap Next, then pick Family Papers.
  6. Mail app settings: Settings › Apps › Mail › Mail Accounts › Fetch New Data › Every 15 Minutes. Gmail can't push to Apple Mail, so new items arrive within 15 minutes, or at once when you open Mail.

Test: in Family Organizer, Settings › This phone › iPhone Calendar and Reminders › Send to iPhone now, then open Mail.

Español: paso 2 para Arlette
  1. Abre Atajos, toca Automatización abajo y luego +.
  2. Elige Correo.
  3. El asunto contiene: [FO-SYNC] Arlette
  4. Cuenta: la cuenta familiapig.
  5. Elige Ejecutar inmediatamente, toca Siguiente y elige Family Papers.
  6. Ajustes › Apps › Mail › Cuentas › Obtener datos › Cada 15 minutos.

Prueba: en Family Organizer, Ajustes › Este móvil › Calendario y Recordatorios del iPhone › Enviar al iPhone ahora y abre Mail.
